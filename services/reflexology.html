Reflexology Therapy

Targeted therapeutic pressure applied to micro-reflex maps on feet and hands to calm the central nervous system, stimulate lymph flow, and ease bodily tension.

Therapy Overview

Restore Circulation & Decompress Your Nervous System

Reflexology is a specialized somatic bodywork technique focused on pressure points mapped across the feet and hands. These micro-reflex zones directly correspond to underlying organ systems, muscular pathways, and nerve networks throughout your entire body.

By systematically stimulating these precise reflex points, your practitioner promotes localized circulation, encourages natural lymphatic drainage, and releases accumulated physical stress. This non-invasive treatment guides your body into deep parasympathetic relaxation, helping reduce pain in the lower extremities while supporting whole-body equilibrium.

Why Choose Reflexology at Green Mantra

  • Deep Parasympathetic Relaxation: Calms central nervous system tension for lasting systemic decompression.
  • Enhanced Lymphatic & Blood Circulation: Promotes healthy fluid movement and reduces leg and foot soreness.
  • Non-Invasive Pain Management: An ideal therapy for managing chronic foot discomfort, joint stiffness, and stress.

Session Guide

What to Expect During Your Appointment

Your reflexology treatment takes place in one of our private, quiet therapy suites. You will sit or recline comfortably in a specialized treatment chair with your feet elevated.

Your reflexologist will begin by cleansing and warming your feet or hands before applying structured, rhythmic pressure to micro-reflex points. Specific zones may feel slightly tender as tension is worked out, followed immediately by a sense of deep physical relief and warmth across your body.
