What to Expect During Your Coaching Journey

Available both in-person at our Pickering sanctuary and virtually via Telehealth, your coaching path begins with an extensive baseline assessment. We evaluate your current sleep quality, stress loads, dietary habits, and biohacking familiarity.

Following your initial strategy session, you will receive a step-by-step roadmap outlining weekly habit shifts.
