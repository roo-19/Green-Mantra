Therapy Overview

Fuel Your Cells with NASA-Engineered MultiWave® Technology

LightStim’s patented MultiWave® Technology delivers multiple therapeutic light spectrums simultaneously—combining Amber, Red, Dark Red, and Near-Infrared wavelengths. This targeted photon energy penetrates deep into tissue, absorbed directly by cytochrome c oxidase inside your mitochondria to supercharge the production of Adenosine Triphosphate (ATP).
