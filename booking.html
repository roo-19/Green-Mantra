Helpful Information

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before stepping into our Pickering wellness sanctuary.

What should I wear to my appointment?

We recommend comfortable, loose-fitting clothing. For Red Light Therapy, direct skin exposure yields optimal cellular benefits, and private treatment drapery is provided. For PEMF and BioCharger, standard casual or athletic wear is ideal.

Can I stack multiple therapies together?

Yes! Therapy stacking is one of our clinical specialties. Combining Bemer PEMF (to optimize microcirculation) directly with LightStim Red Light or NanoVi creates synergistic cellular rejuvenation in a single 60-minute visit.

When should I arrive for my session?

Please arrive 10 minutes prior to your scheduled time to relax in our reception lounge, enjoy fresh alkaline water, and complete any initial intake notes with our coordinator.

What is your cancellation policy?

We kindly ask for at least 24 hours notice for any rescheduling or cancellation. This enables us to offer the reserved suite and practitioner time to guests on our waitlist.
