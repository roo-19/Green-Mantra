Candidate & Associate FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions regarding our practice agreements, facilities, and clinic culture.

What practice and compensation models do you offer?

We offer both competitive split models (ideal for practitioners who want full front-desk, marketing, and linen support) and flat room rental packages for established practitioners who manage their own client roster.

Can I use the biohacking equipment for my own wellness?

Yes! All permanent and part-time practitioners receive complimentary weekly sessions on our LightStim Red Light, Bemer PEMF, and BioCharger systems so you can experience the same restorative benefits as our patients.

What professional registrations are required?

Practitioners offering regulated services (such as RMT, ND, or TCM Acupuncture) must be in active good standing with their respective Ontario regulatory colleges (CMTO, CONO, CTCMPAO) and hold valid professional liability insurance.

How are client referrals managed between practitioners?

Our team meets regularly for collaborative patient case roundtables. When patients present complex needs (e.g. chronic inflammation, post-injury recovery, or gut dysfunction), we coordinate multi-disciplinary care stacks.
